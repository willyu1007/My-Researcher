# Pitfalls

## Do Not Repeat
- Do not treat a hardening task as a new V2 parent roadmap.
- Do not reopen `T-091` closure merely because a post-review improvement exists.
- Do not allow inherited trace refs to masquerade as target-specific writing-affecting trace readiness.
- Do not let broad refs such as board summaries, rationale memos, generic literature refs, or source refs become citation/support authority.
- Do not make live provider variance or real experiment execution a hidden dependency of deterministic T-102 verification.
- Do not reintroduce `research-argument` as an authority path.
