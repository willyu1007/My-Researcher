# Topic Selection — Observed Problems and Desired Improvements

## Purpose and authority

This register preserves recurring product and research-process problems observed while operating the real topic-selection chain, together with the desired behavior and a concrete success signal. It is evidence and requirements input, not a second status, roadmap, architecture, or verification authority. Current scope and sequencing remain in `00-roadmap.md`; settled contracts remain in `02-architecture.md`; decisive checks remain in `verification.md`.

## Improvement discipline

- Solve these issues through the smallest semantic contract that makes the correct behavior unsurprising; do not build a generic agent platform first.
- Full divergence belongs only at research-semantic choices. Deterministic snapshot, publication, package, and gate mechanics remain deterministic.
- Debate creates useful pressure only when participants are genuinely independent; literature/RAG enables correct convergence only when roles inspect claim-bearing evidence. They are one combined quality mechanism.
- Human authority remains exact and snapshot-bound. Faster cadence means fewer meaningless pauses, not weaker control over research meaning, cost, destructive effects, or environment changes.
- An improvement is not complete because a new record or agent exists. It must change stop/continue quality, evidence visibility, risk survival, or researcher control under verification.
