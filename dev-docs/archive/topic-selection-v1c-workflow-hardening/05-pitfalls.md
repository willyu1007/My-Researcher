# Pitfalls

## Do Not Repeat
- Do not treat bridge creation as permission to create PaperProject/PaperImplementation authority.
- Do not let Codex/provider output make promotion decisions.
- Do not mutate historical promotion/bridge artifacts when downstream feedback arrives; append typed recheck signals instead.
- Do not rely on route-only tests as proof that terminal promotion workflow is automation-ready.
- Do not let the compatibility `createPromotionGateSupport` wrapper become the harness acceptance path; N2 support and N3 gate must stay explicit in orchestration evidence.
- Do not collapse split N2/N3 persistence into one ambiguous combined promotion-gate-support count in acceptance manifests.
- Do not leave stale `pending_gaps` in passing manifests after the underlying product gap is closed.
