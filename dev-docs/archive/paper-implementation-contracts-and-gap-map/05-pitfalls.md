# 05 Pitfalls

## Do Not Repeat
- Do not skip the gap map and start with persistence or UI.
- Do not let existing `research-argument` objects become new authority roots.
- Do not classify copied DTOs as reusable without checking ownership and trace semantics.
- Do not treat a missing contract as a product decision; route conflicts back to `T-091`.
- Do not treat T-092 matrices as implemented product behavior.
- Do not re-enable `research-argument` readiness, planner, or writing packet authority through migration wording.
- Do not allow gate, queue, trace, run, claim, or dossier fields required by `08-queryability-field-matrix.md` to become JSON-only in later tasks.
