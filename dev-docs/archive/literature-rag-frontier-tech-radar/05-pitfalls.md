# 05 Pitfalls

## Do-Not-Repeat
- Do not reopen T-125 broad collection after closure.
- Do not batch-promote old READY rows.
- Do not treat candidate rows as collected/effective literature.
- Do not require pure math papers to have RAG mapping cards after D18; instead require repository-aligned theory classification, canonicality or formal-tool value, source stability, and duplicate checks.
- Do not hard-delete duplicate literature records without preserving useful source provenance.
- Do not run destructive DB cleanup without explicit approval.
- Do not rerun a whole B12 tranche for transient embedding SSL failures; extract failed IDs and retry only those IDs with lower concurrency.
- Do not treat raw B10 returns as clean exact-source rows when an arXiv result is not in the selected catalog chunk; unexpected returns must be excluded or separately reviewed.
- Do not fill the memory/context/token stretch target with incidental keyword matches or application-tail rows.
