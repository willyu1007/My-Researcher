# 05 Pitfalls

## Do Not Repeat
- Do not treat generic RAG, generic agent, or generic long-context papers as core unless they include a resource/budget/scheduling/quality-cost-latency decision.
- Do not make theory collection a broad math reading list; every theory item needs a mapping to a RAG/LLM system phenomenon or experimental variable.
- Do not conflate import tags with evidence activation.
- Do not trigger fulltext acquisition, extraction, embedding, or indexing as a side effect of metadata collection.
- Do not assume auto-pull quality score is equivalent to the four-layer research classification.
